## OPEN QUESTIONS FOR OWNER (and lawyer)

Decisions only the owner or a lawyer can make. Nothing below was guessed in the drafts; each draft that depends on one says so.

**For the owner**
- **Q1. No-affiliation line (choose A or B).** Option A keeps the line that is already live: "Proyecto independiente. No es un sitio oficial del BSN." Option B drops it. The footer already carries a longer version approved Sep 24 (it is the live text today). Recommendation: A. Dropping it while using the league's name, club names, and league-sourced data raises the risk of an implied-endorsement complaint. Drafts: L2, L32.
- **Q2. Brand artwork provenance.** The repo does not record who made the original "EL ARCHIVO" mark (`docs/specs/redesign_v3/02_brand_and_logo_spec.md` says you picked `Logo Options/logos-04.png`; its source and the rights to it are not documented). Was it drawn by you, commissioned, or generated by a tool whose terms matter? Is the name "El Archivo" free to use as a brand for this purpose? (Not searched.) Drafts: L53 (left blank until answered).
- **Q3. Licence for your own work.** Who owns, and under what terms may others reuse, the site's design, code, and original text? The repo is public (master plan) and the root has no `LICENSE` file. "All rights reserved" vs. an open licence changes L38. Drafts: L38.
- **Q4. Hosting facts.** The audit verified the site is served from GitHub Pages (`server: GitHub.com`). Do you have any way to see visitor logs or IPs (GitHub Pages does not provide analytics)? Is any DNS/CDN/proxy provider in front of `elarchivopr.com`, and who is the domain registrar (it may also process visitor data)? L16 assumes you receive no visitor data and that GitHub is the only host; confirm.
- **Q5. Delete-everything control.** The app has "Descargar mis datos" and "Restaurar preferencias" but no button that deletes everything stored (streaks, games, saved data copy). L22 therefore tells people to clear site data in their browser. Do you want an in-app button (a build change, not a text change)?
- **Q6. Wikipedia text.** Are the player biographies, team notes and descriptions (`src:'Wikipedia ...'` entries in the code) your own wording, close paraphrases, or copied sentences? Share-alike (CC BY-SA) can apply to adapted text and databases. Also: do you want to offer your compiled data under CC BY-SA, or not? Drafts: L49-L51.
- **Q7. Other sources in Créditos.** By earlier decision RealGM and latinbasket get no new mentions, and the Créditos draft does not list them (they are already named in some data-quality notes). The code also names Primera Hora, El Nuevo Día, El Vocero, Noticel, Telemundo PR, EFE, FIBA, Nuyorican Básquet, PlateaPR, Enciclopedia de Puerto Rico and the Salón de la Fama del Deporte de Río Piedras in per-player source lines. List them all in Créditos (completeness) or keep the generic sentence (L52)? Should the Internet Archive (Wayback Machine) be credited for the pre-2021 bsnpr.com tables?
- **Q8. Separate `#cookies` page?** The redesign spec lists a cookies/storage page; your PHASE_69 request has four pages. The privacy draft includes the full storage table (L19). Keep it folded in (recommended, there are no cookies) or add a fifth page?
- **Q9. Contact identity.** The contact is a personal Gmail and an Instagram handle; no legal entity or address is claimed (settled Oct 9). Do you want your own name anywhere in the Terms? (Not required by the drafts.) Master plan item: check your employer's outside-business-activities policy before accounts, giveaways or income.
- **Q10. Money.** L36 says the site sells nothing and shows no ads. True today. Any ads, sponsors, store or paid promotion changes Terms, Privacy and disclosure text.
- **Q11. Accessibility promises.** L46 says you will answer reports; do you want to commit to a response time? (Draft does not.) Fill in the date of the last review once PHASE_71 is done.
- **Q12. Footer and zoom (non-legal approvals tied to this phase).** (a) Footer text change (L3). (b) Italic: ship no italic font; the single `.nodata` label would render with a synthesised slant. (c) Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=no` from the viewport tag so people can zoom (WCAG 1.4.4); layout re-check required.

**For a lawyer**
- **Q13. Whole-text review** of L13-L56 (Privacidad, Términos, Accesibilidad, Créditos) before publication.
- **Q14. Trademarks and logos.** The site names the league and its clubs, draws club emblems/mascots in club colours (original drawings, no league files; `tabs.js:609`, `:3469`), and links to the league's channels. Is that acceptable nominative use? Is the no-affiliation line (option A) enough? The league was emailed Sep 22 with no reply; their answer overrides these drafts.
- **Q15. League-sourced material.** The site shows summarised league-app headlines (`tabs.js:1509`), a standings capture (`tabs.js:1573`), and 2026 totals derived from the league's official player export. Is reuse permitted? Statistics are facts, but compilations can be protected (`docs/project.md` L1).
- **Q16. Image and likeness rights.** No photos are used today; player pages use initials on a colour gradient and named individuals appear with career statistics. Any future photo, and any use of a living person's name in a sponsored or monetised context, needs a separate review.
- **Q17. Puerto Rico duties if data is ever collected (PHASE_70).** Once emails or accounts exist: what notification duties (data breach, privacy notice, consumer-data rules) apply to a PR-based operator with no legal entity, and are there duties to PR residents or US-federal rules for minors? Not addressed in these drafts because they describe a site that collects nothing.
- **Q18. 13+ age gate for PHASE_70.** Is "13 años o más" the right line, and how should it be asked and stored? Not drafted here.
- **Q19. Visitors outside PR/US.** The site is Spanish-language and PR-focused. Do EU/UK visitor rules matter at all if the site targets PR? (Spec says keep non-essential tracking off and the question disappears.)
- **Q20. Governing law, venue, and the limitation-of-liability wording** in L37. The draft deliberately does not name a governing law or court.
- **Q21. Takedown/removal procedure** (L39) and what the owner promises on timing.
- **Q22. Whether a no-entity operator should state who the "operator" is** in the Terms (L34 uses the brand "bennizpr" as the spec requires).
